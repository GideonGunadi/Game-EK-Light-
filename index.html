🔦 Find the Magic Note!

The room is super dark! Move your mouse to aim the flashlight. Move the mouse near the left or right edge of the screen to turn your head. There is one sticky note on the board that says “light”. Find it and click it!

Press F = flashlight, L = room light. The flashlight starts off, so turn it on first!
